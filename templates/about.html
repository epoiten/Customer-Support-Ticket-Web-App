Business Problem & Solution

The Business Problem: In acute hospital settings (surgical wards and ICUs), equipment malfunctions (ranging from infusion pump occlusion alarms to vital monitor sensor faults and EHR workstation glitches) are often reported via unstructured phone calls, paper slips, or fragmented messaging apps. This leads to delayed biomedical engineering triage, lost fault records, and increased patient safety risks due to unverified medical devices.

The Software Solution: The MedTech Support Desk is a lightweight, responsive web application built to centralize incident reporting and tracking. It enables nursing and clinical staff to log equipment faults, monitor repair triage states (Open, In Progress, Resolved), update clinical notes, and securely delete resolved records, ensuring clinical engineering teams maintain full auditability over ward technology.

Main Functionality & Technologies

  • Service Overview Portal: Landing page introducing the biomedical helpdesk with live aggregate ticket statistics.
  • Incident Creation Form: Structured form capturing staff identity/ward, hospital email, device asset subject, and fault descriptions.
  • Persistent Storage: Relational SQLite database (tickets.db) managed via Peewee ORM with a dedicated Ticket table.
  • Ticket Queue & CRUD Operations: Responsive tabular view with color-coded status badges, detail pages, editing forms, and secure deletion routes.
  • Tech Stack: Python 3.12, Flask, Peewee ORM, HTML5 (Jinja2), CSS3 (responsive grid), and Vanilla JavaScript for character counting and form validation.

Limitations & Future Improvements

Current Limitations: Lack of user authentication/RBAC (any user can edit/delete tickets in this prototype); single database table storing static text notes without a historical audit log.

Future Improvements: Implementing Flask-Login with distinct roles for clinical staff and biomedical technicians, automated notifications for critical status changes, and database table normalization (Users, Wards, Equipment).
